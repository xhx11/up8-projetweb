Ivan JIN

About the artist

Occupation : Model

Born in 2004, Ivan JIN is a French-Chinese model. Standing at 185 cm, he combines a sensitivity shaped by Chinese culture with a contemporary approach to European fashion.

Known for his strong presence in front of the camera and his versatility, Ivan works across a wide range of aesthetics, from minimalist and experimental projects to outdoor-inspired fashion editorials. His work is characterized by an ability to convey different moods and visual identities.

In recent years, Ivan JIN has collaborated with several French independent designers and brands, participating in fashion campaigns and creative projects. Alongside his modeling career, he is also interested in cinema and has taken part in various independent audiovisual productions.

Drawing on his multicultural background, Ivan JIN continues to develop a practice influenced by diverse cultural perspectives, pursuing his path between fashion, image-making, and contemporary creation.
