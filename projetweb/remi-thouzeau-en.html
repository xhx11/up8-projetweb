Rémi Thouzeau

About the artist

Occupation : Painter

Born in 2002, Rémi Thouzeau explores fine oil painting and the boundaries between reality and imagination. His work reveals a subtle tension between softness and mystery, with silent scenes inhabited by enigmatic figures where every detail feels meaningful.

Since 2022, he has presented several solo exhibitions in Paris, including Instant T (2022), Heavy Dreams (2023) and Confidence des opposés (2024). He is currently preparing his next show.

His work has also been shown at the Guardian Art Center in Beijing and in the Artist Rooms Paris spaces he co-founded. As Director of Public Relations at AAEC, he fosters dialogue between the European and Asian artistic scenes.
