Aurélien Baudoin

About the artist

Occupation : Designer

Aurélien Baudoin is a textile designer based in Paris. Through his "chemise.verte" project, he reuses fabric scraps from fashion houses to create limited series pieces, from small accessories to recomposed clothing.

His work combines local manufacturing, artisanal technique, and a contemporary approach to clothing, while emphasizing material reuse and craftsmanship.

His creations have been shown in Paris and beyond. He has presented work at the Guardian Art Center in Beijing and in the Artist Rooms Paris spaces, which he co-founded. As Director of Public Relations at the AAEC, he fosters dialogue between the European and Asian artistic scenes.
